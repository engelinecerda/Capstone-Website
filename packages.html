A signed contract and initial downpayment are required to confirm your reservation. Dates are only secured upon receipt of both.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be submitted at least 7 days before your event date. Cancellations within 7 days may forfeit the downpayment.

Changes to the final guest count must be communicated at least 48 hours before the event. Last-minute additions may not be accommodated.

Duration & Extensions

All packages include setup and breakdown time within the stated hours. Extension fees apply if the event exceeds the booked duration.

Customization Requests

Special requests, theme adjustments, or menu modifications should be communicated at least 3 days prior to the event.
