Required Fields

Most fields on the booking form (package, guest count, event date) drive pricing and availability and can't be made optional. These are the only fields that can be toggled.

Phone number required
If off, customers can submit a reservation with just an email address.
Special requests required
If on, customers must write something in the Special Requests field before continuing.

Terms & Legal

Terms & Conditions and Data Privacy Policy appear on the booking page before customers sign their contract; Cancellation Policy and Reschedule Policy appear on the My Reservations page before a customer confirms a cancellation or reschedule request. All four use the same format: blank-line-separated paragraphs, a short line with no ending period becomes a heading, and lines starting with "- " become a bullet list.

This text also feeds the contract via the {{terms_and_conditions}} and {{data_privacy_policy}} tokens on the Contract Template tab — editing it here updates any clause that references those tokens too.

Shown in the "Important Notice" box customers see before confirming a cancellation on the My Reservations page — right above the cancellation fee amount.
