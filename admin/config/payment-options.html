Payment Deadline and Grace Period

When an approved reservation's payment is due, and how long it can stay unpaid after that before it is cancelled automatically. A reservation with no payment at all is cancelled without a cancellation fee; one that was partly paid is cancelled and charged the cancellation fee set in Payment Rules above.

Payment due date

The full balance is due this many days before the event. This applies to every unpaid reservation, including existing ones, so raising it can make some of them overdue right away. Keep it shorter than your minimum booking notice, otherwise new bookings would already be late.

days before the event

Grace period after the due date

Extra days customers get after the payment due date. Set to 0 to cancel as soon as the due date passes. Reservations with a payment waiting for review are never cancelled while it is pending.

days

Service Charge

A percentage added to the package total to cover utilities and staff. Shown to customers as its own line — never folded into the package price. Applies to the package and add-on total only; there is no separate travel-fee line item to include, so nothing is excluded on that basis.

Apply service charge to offsite packages

When off, offsite events are not charged the service charge. When on, they follow the same rate as onsite, including category overrides.
